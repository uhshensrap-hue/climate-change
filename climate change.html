What is Climate Change?

Climate Change refers to long-term changes in Earth's temperature and weather patterns. Human activities are a major cause of climate change.

Causes of Climate Change

  • Deforestation
  • Air Pollution
  • Burning of Fossil Fuels
  • Industrial Activities
  • Increasing Population

Effects of Climate Change

  • Global Warming
  • Melting of Ice
  • Rising Sea Levels
  • Extreme Weather
  • Loss of Wildlife

Solutions to Climate Change

  • Plant More Trees
  • Save Electricity
  • Use Solar Energy
  • Reduce Plastic
  • Save Water
